
# Maize Stalks and Composting

## Category
Crop Residue Management

## Topic
Using maize stalks as composting material

## Overview
Maize stalks, also known as maize stover, are agricultural
residues that can be reused instead of being discarded.
One possible use is incorporating them into compost.

## Preparing maize stalks
Large and coarse materials such as maize stalks should
be chopped into smaller pieces before being added to
a compost heap.

Smaller pieces provide more surface area for
decomposition.

## Materials used in composting
Composting can involve a combination of:
- Dry plant materials such as maize stover and straw.
- Green plant materials such as grass and vegetable waste.
- Animal manure.
- Water to maintain moisture.

## Composting process
Organic materials can be arranged in layers when
preparing a compost heap.

The materials should have sufficient moisture to
support microbial activity.

Composting allows microorganisms to break down
organic matter over time.

## Important precautions
- Avoid adding plastic and other non-biodegradable waste.
- Avoid diseased or poisonous plant materials.
- Avoid dog and cat manure.
- Protect compost from excessive drying and waterlogging.

## Relevance to FarmLoop
Maize stalks can be listed on FarmLoop as agricultural
residues that may be useful to farmers and compost
producers.

Their suitability depends on their condition, preparation
and intended use.
